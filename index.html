How Do We Work Together?

A simple resource for understanding how coworkers are wired, where connection may come naturally, where tension may show up, and how to work together better.

When tension shows up, get curious.

This tool is not here to tell you who is right or wrong. It looks across the information we know about both people—personality, strengths, work style, communication, spiritual gifts, temperament, and appreciation—to surface patterns that may help explain the relationship.

Choose two people and click Compare.

How to use this

Use it before a hard conversation, after a confusing interaction, or when you are trying to understand why two good people keep missing each other.

1. Assume the bestDifferent wiring is not bad character. Start with curiosity before judgment.
2. Name the difference“I think we may be approaching this differently” is more useful than “you always...”
3. Have the tough conversation and season it with graceUse the insight to create clarity, not ammunition.
4. Ask what support would helpClear trumps cute. End with a practical next step.
The insights are designed to create curiosity and clearer conversations, not labels, diagnoses, or guarantees.
